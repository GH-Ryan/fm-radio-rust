FM Radio 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-11

一句话总结：FM Radio 不收集、不上传、不共享、不出售任何个人信息。所有需要记住的内容 （你的收藏、你上次播放的电台、你选择的界面语言）只保存在你自己的设备上。

1. 我们不收集什么

本应用没有账号体系，不要求你登录，也不会生成任何用于识别你的标识。具体来说，本应用：

  • 不创建、不要求、也不存储任何用户账号；
  • 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话号码、地址、精确或粗略位置；
  • 不收集设备标识符、广告标识符（如 Windows 广告 ID）；
  • 不收集崩溃报告、性能遥测或使用分析；
  • 不出售、不出租、不共享你的任何信息给第三方。

应用中没有任何统计分析、广告或崩溃上报 SDK。开发者无法知道你是否安装了本应用、 是否使用过它，也无法知道你播放过哪些电台。

2. 保存在你设备上的内容

本应用会把你主动操作产生的内容保存在你自己设备的应用私有数据目录中。保存这些内容是为了让应用在下次启动时恢复你的使用状态。这些内容始终留在你的设备上，不会被上传到任何服务器，也不会自动同步到其他设备或账号。

内容何时产生存放位置
你收藏的电台列表你点击收藏按钮时应用私有数据目录中的 favorites.json
你上次播放的电台你播放电台时应用私有数据目录中的 last.json
界面语言偏好需要时应用的 WebView 本地存储中，键名 fmradio.locale

如何删除这些内容

  • 删除某一条收藏：在应用内移除该收藏即可。
  • 删除全部本地数据：卸载本应用。Windows 会一并删除其私有数据目录。
  • 也可以在 Windows 的「设置 → 应用 → 已安装的应用 → FM Radio → 高级选项」中清除应用数据。

因为这些数据从未离开过你的设备，删除它们不需要联系任何人，也不存在需要等待的保留期。

传输加密

应用与 Radio Browser 之间的通信使用 HTTPS，包括你输入的搜索关键词。

但电台音频流由各运营方自行提供，其中部分老旧电台仍使用明文 HTTP。 这是运营方的技术选择，应用不强制升级为 HTTPS，也无法代替运营方作出这项保证。 因此，若你在不可信的网络环境下收听广播，建议自行使用可信的 VPN。

4. 第三方服务

本应用依赖以下第三方服务，它们各自有自己的隐私政策：

  • Radio Browser —— 公开的电台目录，无注册要求。
  • 各家广播电台运营方 —— 音频流与图标的实际提供方。

本应用无法控制上述第三方如何处理其收到的连接信息。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。由于本应用不收集任何个人信息，该限制不会带来额外的实际风险。

6. 本政策的变更

In one line: FM Radio does not collect, upload, share, or sell any personal information. Everything it needs to remember — your favourites, the station you last played, and your interface language — stays on your own device.

1. What we do not collect

This app has no account system, never asks you to sign in, and generates no identifier that could be used to recognise you. Specifically, the app:

  • does not create, require, or store any user account;
  • does not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or location;
  • does not collect device identifiers or advertising identifiers (such as the Windows advertising ID);
  • does not collect crash reports, performance telemetry, or usage analytics;
  • does not sell, rent, or share any information about you with third parties.

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK. The developer cannot tell whether you installed the app, whether you used it, or which stations you played.

2. What is stored on your device

When you act on the app, the result is saved in the app's private data folder on your own device. This exists so the app can restore your state on next launch. This data stays on your device and is never uploaded to any server, and it is never synced to another device or account.

DataCreated whenStored in
Your list of favourite stationsYou mark a station as a favouritefavorites.json in the app's private data folder
The station you last playedYou play a stationlast.json in the app's private data folder
Your interface languageWhen one is recordedThe app's WebView local storage, under the key fmradio.locale

How to delete it

  • Remove one favourite: remove it in the app.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the app. Windows deletes its private data folder with it.
  • You can also clear it from Settings → Apps → Installed apps → FM Radio → Advanced options.

Because this data never left your device, deleting it requires contacting no one and there is no retention period to wait out.

3. Network requests the app makes

The app needs a network connection to play radio. To that end it contacts:

PartyWhat is sentWhat they can see
Radio Browser
(*.api.radio-browser.info)		 Station catalogue requests, and the search terms you type into the search box Your IP address, your User-Agent, and the search terms you entered. Nothing that identifies you personally.
The broadcaster of the station you choose to play The audio stream connection Your IP address and User-Agent. The broadcaster's own logs may record this; their policy is outside this app's control.
The broadcaster, or an image host / CDN it uses Requests for station logo images Your IP address and User-Agent.

Please note: stations are operated by independent third parties. The app cannot make commitments on their behalf, and makes no request to any broadcaster until you choose to play.

Encryption in transit

Communication between the app and Radio Browser uses HTTPS, including the search terms you type.

Audio streams, however, are supplied by each broadcaster, and some older stations still serve plain HTTP. That is the operator's technical choice; the app neither upgrades nor can guarantee it on their behalf. If you listen on an untrusted network, use a VPN you trust.

4. Third-party services

The app relies on the following, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Radio Browser — a public, registration-free radio directory.
  • Individual radio broadcasters — the actual providers of audio streams and images.

This app cannot control how those third parties handle the connection information they receive.

5. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects no personal information at all, this introduces no additional practical risk.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the change will be announced in the Microsoft Store listing's update notes and the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Continuing to use the app means you accept the updated policy.
